Is 10Demo worth $149/mo when I could hire a sales rep for $150K/year?

One mid-market SDR runs ~25 demos a week. 10Demo runs unlimited concurrent demos in 21+ languages, 24/7, and surfaces qualified leads only. The math holds at any team size.

How is 10Demo different from Consensus or Demostack?

Those are static demo platforms — recorded screens with click-to-advance. 10Demo is a live AI agent that adapts to each prospect, qualifies them in the conversation, and books the meeting in the same call.

Does the AI sound natural or will prospects know it's not human?

The AI is upfront — it identifies itself. What customers report is that prospects prefer it: zero scheduling friction, instant answers, and never a sales-pitch detour.

Can I customize how the AI presents demos and responds to prospects?

Yes — knowledge base, tone, qualification rules, booking logic, and CRM hooks are all configurable.

Can AI really handle technical questions and objections?

It handles 90%+ of inbound technical questions in customer deployments. For the rest, it routes the prospect to the right rep with a brief.
